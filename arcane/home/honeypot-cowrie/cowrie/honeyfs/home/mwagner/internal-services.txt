quick reference: reaching the dc01/bastion02 estate from outside VLAN10
(nobody ever documented this properly, writing it down before I forget again)

gw does NAT port-forwarding for the directory/AI-tooling boxes, same public
IP as everything else, just different ports -- ops never split them onto
their own external address:

  bastion02 (ssh, secondary admin bastion)........ gw:2200
  dc01 (ldap, directory services)................. gw:389
  agent-gateway-01 (mcp, AI-agent tooling gw)...... gw:8000
  cms-legacy-01 (http, old CMS nobody retired)..... gw:8880

analytics-es-04 (standalone ES node, analytics team's own instance, NOT the
same as es-logs-01 in /opt/nexusai-inference/.env) is on VLAN12, same
NAT-forward setup, just port 9201.
